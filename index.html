Kevin Issac

I am an ELLIS/ELIZA Doctoral Researcher in the Visual Inference Lab at TU Darmstadt, supervised by Stefan Roth. My research focuses on computer vision and machine learning, with an emphasis on ill-posed problems such as sparse-view scene synthesis.

I obtained my Master’s degree in Visual Computing from Saarland University. During my Master’s, I was supervised by Jan Eric Lenssen at the Max Planck Institute for Informatics, Germany. Prior to that, I worked as a Research Assistant at the Spectrum Lab, Indian Institute of Science (IISc), where I conducted research in biomedical image processing under the supervision of Chandra Sekhar Seelamantula and Harish Kumar JR.
